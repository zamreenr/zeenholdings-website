How it works

Two audiences, one connection

Rather than growing or processing tea ourselves, we act as the trusted link between Sri Lankan suppliers and buyers abroad — sourcing tea on the local side, and opening the door to buyers on the European side.

For buyers

International Buyers

Based in Germany, we work directly with buyers across Germany and wider Europe — understanding what European importers need, from documentation to delivery expectations, and sourcing accordingly from Sri Lanka.

For suppliers

Local Suppliers

In Sri Lanka, we're building relationships with tea suppliers and estates to source quality product for export — giving smaller suppliers a route to international buyers they may not otherwise reach.

Where we are today

An honest starting point

Zeen Holdings' tea export line is newly underway — we don't yet have fixed grades or specialty lines locked in, because our model is to source according to what buyers actually need, rather than pushing a fixed catalogue. If you're a buyer with specific requirements, or a supplier with tea to move, we'd rather have that conversation directly.

Our story

A family business, built across two generations and two countries.

Zeen Holdings Lanka (Pvt) Ltd was founded in 2022 as a family venture, with operations gradually ramping up since. What makes us different isn't a large office or a long history — it's the combination of two very different kinds of experience under one roof.

30+ years in cargo

Our director

Our director has spent more than three decades in the cargo industry, building the relationships with airlines, agents and customs authorities that make dependable freight forwarding possible. That network — not a brochure — is the real foundation of the freight forwarding side of the business.

European vantage point

The next generation

Based in Germany as an engineer, the second generation brings modern tools and direct exposure to European buyers and standards — helping shape how the company runs, and giving Zeen Holdings a genuine foothold in both Sri Lanka and Europe rather than relying on a chain of go-betweens.

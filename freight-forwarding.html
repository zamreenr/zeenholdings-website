Core service

Air Freight

Our primary strength — with direct relationships across multiple airlines, giving us flexible capacity and competitive routing for time-sensitive cargo.

Sea Freight

Licensed customs brokerage alongside import and export clearance, handled locally, so your cargo isn't held up by paperwork or classification issues on either end of the journey.

Import & Export

Full experience moving cargo in both directions — not just outbound shipments from Sri Lanka, but inbound freight as well.
